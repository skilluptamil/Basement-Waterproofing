Core Service 01

Basement Waterproofing

Complete sub-floor perimeter French drain tile, heavy-duty vapor barrier wall shields, and dual sump pump ejection designed to relieve destructive hydrostatic pressure permanently.

Common Problems & Warning Signs:

  • Water seeping through floor-wall cove joints during rainstorms
  • Chalky white efflorescence salt deposits on basement walls
  • Damp, musty humidity spoiling finished carpeting and drywall

Our Engineered Waterproofing Protocol:

We excavate a continuous 12-inch trench along the interior footing, embed perforated schedule 40 pipe within washed drainage aggregate, install semi-rigid vapor barrier wall panels, and connect directly to a dual-pump cast-iron sump station.

25-Year Transferable Warranty Turnaround: 2-3 Days

Core Service 02

Foundation Crack Repair

High-pressure structural epoxy and hydrophobic polyurethane injection paired with aerospace-grade carbon fiber grid straps to halt water ingress and restore monolithic wall strength.

Common Problems & Warning Signs:

  • Horizontal fractures indicating inward soil pressure and wall bowing
  • Diagonal stair-step cracks across block mortar seams
  • Leaking groundwater trickling through foundation fractures

Our Engineered Repair Protocol:

Calibrated injection ports are bonded along the fracture. We inject high-pressure hydrophobic resin that expands up to 20x its liquid volume, penetrating the entire depth of the wall. Carbon-fiber grid straps are thermal-epoxy bonded to permanently arrest lateral deflection.

Lifetime Structural Bond Warranty Turnaround: 1 Day

Core Service 03

Foundation Stabilization

Hydraulic steel push piers and helical tieback systems to permanently arrest foundation settlement and lift sinking residential structures back toward original grade.

Common Problems & Warning Signs:

  • Interior doors and windows sticking or refusing to latch properly
  • Diagonal cracks spreading through drywall, ceiling corners, and exterior bricks
  • Uneven, sloping floors upstairs indicating footing settlement

Our Engineered Piering Protocol:

Heavy-duty galvanized steel brackets are anchored to the foundation footing. Hydraulic rams drive tubular steel pier columns deep through unstable soil until reaching refusal in solid bedrock (typically 15-45 feet). Synchronized hydraulic jacks gently lift settled corners.

Lifetime Level Structural Warranty PE Stamped Calculations

Core Service 04

Basement Leak Repair

Rapid-response localized leak detection, tie-rod hole sealing, pipe penetration gaskets, and pressure-relief conduits to stop active flowing water immediately.

Common Problems & Warning Signs:

  • Active water trickling through concrete form tie-rod holes during storms
  • Water bubbling up through basement floor control joints under pressure
  • Seepage around sewer lines, gas entries, or basement window wells

Our Engineered Water Stop Protocol:

We use acoustic and thermal detection to trace leak channels. We mechanically route the fissure into a dovetail keyway and inject rapid-curing hydrophilic polyurethane that seals actively flowing water within 60 seconds.

10-Year Water Stop Bond 24/7 Emergency Dispatch Available

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Complete 20-mil reinforced antimicrobial vapor barrier encapsulation, foundation wall sealing, joist rot prevention, and commercial digital dehumidification.

Drainage Systems

Engineered exterior curtain drains, underground rigid PVC downspout conductors, catch basins, and pop-up lawn emitters that divert roof storm runoff far away from foundation walls.

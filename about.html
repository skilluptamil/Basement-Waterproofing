Our Heritage

Built on Engineering Rigor and Integrity

When founder David Vance, PE, started TitanShield over two decades ago, he recognized a troubling industry standard: contractors offering cheap, superficial epoxy smears that cracked again the following winter.

He established TitanShield on a different foundation: every project must begin with mathematical geotechnical analysis of soil expansive pressures, and every repair must be engineered to outlast the home itself. Today, with over 12,500 basements stabilized, we remain an independent, engineer-led practice.

Quality Assurance

The 5-Stage Protocol

How every home progresses from initial inspection to certified lifetime warranty deed.

  1. Stage 1 - Site Discovery: Multi-sensor laser elevation scan and wall plumb alignment check.
  2. Stage 2 - Soil & Hydrology Plan: Stamped calculation of lateral clay pressure and footing load capacity.
  3. Stage 3 - Home Protection: Heavy plastic containment walls and negative air scrubbers set up.
  4. Stage 4 - Precision Build: High-pressure resin injection, steel piers driven to bedrock, or French drain install.
  5. Stage 5 - Engineering Sign-Off: Laser verification of zero movement, water flow test, warranty certificate issued.

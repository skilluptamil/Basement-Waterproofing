Our Heritage

Built on Engineering Rigor and Integrity

When founder David Vance, PE, started TitanShield over two decades ago, he recognized a troubling industry standard: contractors offering cheap, superficial epoxy smears that cracked again the following winter.

He established TitanShield on a different foundation: every project must begin with mathematical geotechnical analysis of soil expansive pressures, and every repair must be engineered to outlast the home itself. Today, with over 12,500 basements stabilized, we remain an independent, engineer-led practice.

Quality Assurance

The 5-Stage Protocol

How every home progresses from initial inspection to certified lifetime warranty deed.

01

Stage 1: Multi-Sensor Site Discovery

Multi-sensor laser elevation scan, wall plumb alignment check, thermal imaging, and electronic crack mapping to accurately quantify sub-millimeter foundation deflection.

02

Stage 2: Soil & Hydrology Engineering Plan

Stamped calculation of lateral expansive clay pressure, water table hydrostatic head, and footing load-bearing capacity by licensed Professional Engineers.

03

Stage 3: Clean Home Containment & Protection

Heavy-gauge sealed plastic containment barriers, floor protection runners, and commercial negative-air HEPA scrubbers set up before any tool touches concrete.

04

Stage 4: Precision Engineered Build

High-pressure structural resin injection, hydraulic steel piers driven to load-bearing bedrock, or laser-pitched sub-floor French drain installation with clean aggregate.

05

Stage 5: Final Engineering Sign-Off & Warranty

Final laser telemetry verification confirming zero structural deflection, high-volume water evacuation test, and issuance of the 25-Year Transferable Warranty Deed.
